Welcome to Cryptocurrencies

What is Crypto? Cryptocurrency or often associated with shorter name "crypto" is a digital currency that utilizes cryptography for secure transactions and is distributed on a decentralized network. It is a form of digital asset that can be used for payments or as a store of value. Unlike traditional currencies backed by central banks, cryptocurrencies rely on blockchain, a public ledger that records all transactions.
