Beginner’s Guide

Confuse Where To Begin? Follow The Path

  1. Get to Know Crypto
    Cryptocurrency is digital money that operates on blockchain technology—secure, decentralized, and includes Bitcoin (BTC) and Ethereum (ETH).
  2. Get a Wallet
    You’ll need a crypto wallet to store your assets.
    • Hot wallets (connected to internet; less secure)
    • Cold wallets (offline; more secure)
  3. Choose a Trusted Exchange
    Use reliable platforms with strong security and transparent fees.
  4. Start Small
    Invest only what you can afford to lose.
  5. Do Your Own Research (DYOR)
    Understand coins and avoid hype.
  6. Stay Safe
    Use 2FA and don’t share your keys.
  7. Think Long Term
    Crypto is a long-term investment.
Tips & Tricks

Need Some Cool Advice? We Got Some!

  1. Start with the Basics
    • Know the Basics: Understand blockchain and key coins.
    • Understand Volatility: Prices can swing wildly—prepare for risks.
  2. Diversify Your Portfolio
    • Don't put all your eggs in one basket—mix stable and promising altcoins.
  3. Set Realistic Expectations
    • Be prepared for losses, and take profits wisely.
  4. Don’t FOMO (Fear of Missing Out)
    • Stick to your strategy and avoid emotion-based decisions.
  5. Have an Exit Strategy
    • Know when to sell and plan ahead.
